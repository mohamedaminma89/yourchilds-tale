Privacy Policy for YourChild's Tale

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who we are

YourChild's Tale is a mobile app that helps parents and guardians create personalized narrated stories for children. The app is operated by Mohamed Amin and can be contacted at momorok45@hotmail.com.

Who may use the app

The app is intended to be used by parents and guardians for family storytelling. Children should use the app only with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian. During account creation, the parent or guardian confirms that they consent to the processing of the child profile information used to create stories.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Parent account information, such as email address and authentication details.
  • Child profile information provided by the parent, such as first name, age, and selected profile options.
  • Story inputs, such as selected language, theme, story length, and chosen children.
  • Generated story content, narration audio, and story library history.
  • Optional voice recordings if a parent chooses to create a custom voice for private family narration.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as app requests, device type, and error logs.
  • Subscription or purchase status if paid features are enabled in the future.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create personalized stories and narration audio.
  • Save recent stories in the family library.
  • Provide account sign-in, customer support, and account deletion.
  • Improve reliability, fix bugs, prevent abuse, and secure the service.
  • Process optional voice cloning only when requested by the parent or guardian.
  • Check access to paid or test features when applicable.

Service providers we use

We share data only with service providers needed to run the app. These providers process data on our behalf and may include:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage.
  • OpenAI and Google Cloud for story generation, text processing, and text-to-speech features.
  • Modal or another voice infrastructure provider for optional custom voice generation.
  • Render for backend hosting.
  • Google Play services for app distribution, purchases, crash reporting, and store operations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use children's personal information for third-party advertising.

Voice recordings and custom voices

Custom voice is optional. If a parent records or uploads a voice sample, it is used to create private family narration inside the app. Voice data should not be uploaded without the consent of the person whose voice is being recorded. Parents can delete a custom voice from the app settings or contact us for help.

Children's privacy

We collect child profile information only when provided by a parent or guardian for the purpose of creating personalized stories. We aim to collect only what is needed for the storytelling experience. Parents can request access, correction, or deletion of their family's data at any time.

Data retention and deletion

We keep account, profile, story, and audio data for as long as needed to provide the service, unless the parent deletes it or asks us to delete it. The app includes account deletion in Settings. Account deletion removes the account and associated stories, voice clone data, and subscription data where technically possible.

You can also request deletion by emailing momorok45@hotmail.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect user data, including authenticated access to accounts and backend services. No internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to limit access to data and improve protections over time.

International processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than where you live. By using the app, you understand that data may be processed internationally where our providers operate their services.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your personal data. Parents and guardians may exercise these rights for their family account by using the app settings or contacting us.

Contact

For privacy questions, account deletion, or data requests, contact: momorok45@hotmail.com.
