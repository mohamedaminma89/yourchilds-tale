Support and account deletion

For help with YourChild's Tale, account access, privacy requests, or deletion, contact momorok45@hotmail.com.

Delete your account in the app

Open the app, sign in, go to Settings, then choose the account deletion option. This permanently deletes your account and associated stories, voice clone data, and subscription data where technically possible.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the same address used for your account and write "Delete my YourChild's Tale account" in the subject line. We may ask for information needed to verify the request before deleting data.

Plans

The first 10 stories are free. Premium includes up to 40 stories per month, and the voice of each story can be changed up to 3 times.

Nurseries, kids clubs and schools

Premium is designed for families. If you run a nursery, kids club, school, or another organisation, email momorok45@hotmail.com and we will prepare a custom plan.

Response time

We aim to respond to support and deletion requests within 7 days.
